MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is entered into by and between:
Party A: Tech Corp (registered in United States)
Party B: Innovate Ltd (registered in India)

1. PURPOSE & OBJECTIVES
The parties intend to collaborate on developing artificial intelligence software models. This agreement outlines their shared vision and general intent.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep all proprietary information shared during this collaboration strictly confidential. This Section 2 shall be legally binding upon the parties.

3. GOVERNING LAW
This MOU shall be governed by the laws of New York, USA. This Section 3 shall be legally binding.

4. NON-BINDING NATURE
Except for Sections 2 and 3, this MOU is non-binding and represents only a statement of intent to cooperate. Neither party shall be liable for failing to enter into a definitive agreement.
